Full description:
Trivia Night: Big Screen turns a phone or tablet into the host's console for an in-person trivia night. Cast or mirror the screen to a TV and the whole room sees each question as it comes up. The answer stays hidden until you tap Show Answer.

Setting up a game takes about a minute:
- Pick any of 15 categories: History, Science, Television, Pop Culture, Movies & Entertainment, Music, Geography, Sports, Literature, Art & Architecture, Food & Drink, Animals & Nature, Technology, Mythology, and Words & Language.
- Choose how many questions to play.
- Set the difficulty from 1 (most people will know it) to 10 (only serious trivia buffs will get it), or give each category its own level.
- Set a countdown of 10 to 120 seconds per question.

During the game, questions rotate through your categories so one subject doesn't run several times in a row. Previous and Next move between questions, and the answer is hidden again every time a question comes back on screen. A keyboard, remote, or game controller works too: arrows move, and Space or Enter shows the answer.

The app ships with 3,300 questions and remembers which ones it has already shown on your device. Nothing repeats until the questions in your chosen categories run out, and you can reset that history whenever you like.

Scoring stays on paper or however your group likes to keep it. The app works offline, has no ads, and collects no data.
